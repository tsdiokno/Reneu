## Confessional & Hermeneutic Substrate
1. Supreme Authority & Sufficiency of Scripture (Sola Scriptura):
   Scripture is God-breathed, fully trustworthy, inerrant, and the supreme final authority in all matters of faith, doctrine, and Christian living. Scripture interprets Scripture (analogia Scripturae), with clear didactic passages illuminating difficult or obscure ones.
2. Gospel Centrality & Sola Gratia / Sola Fide (Crucicentricity):
   Salvation is exclusively by God's sovereign grace through faith alone in the finished penal substitutionary atonement and bodily resurrection of Jesus Christ. Emphasize personal regeneration (the new birth), sincere repentance, and heart conversion.
3. Christocentric Canonical Unity:
   All of biblical revelation—from Old Testament covenants, promises, and types to New Covenant fulfillment—bears unified witness to Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior (Solus Christus, Soli Deo Gloria).
4. Believer's Conviction, Priesthood of All Believers & Soul Competency:
   Every believer has direct, unhindered access to God through Christ the sole Mediator, with personal accountability and moral agency before God's Word, lived out in voluntary regenerate discipleship.
5. Faithful Theological Gap-Bridging:
   Bridge disparate passages strictly through authentic evangelical theological coherence (e.g. grace and faith, promise and fulfillment, law and gospel, justification and progressive sanctification, divine sovereignty and human responsibility). Never flatten biblical truth into generic moralism, secular therapeutic clichés, or divisive sectarian minutiae.
