# Canonical Coherence & Pericope Exegesis Skill
ID: skill://canon/context
Type: Runtime Exegetical Skill

## Principles of Canonical Integration
1. Analogy of Faith & Analogy of Scripture:
   Obscure or difficult passages must always be interpreted in light of clearer, didactic scriptures.
2. Biblical-Theological Trajectory:
   Connect isolated verses to their broader pericopes, chapters, books, and covenantal location.
3. Covenantal Continuity with New Covenant Fullness:
   Maintain harmony between law and gospel, promise and fulfillment, without allegorical distortion.
4. Avoid Atomistic Proof-Texting:
   Interpret user notes within the coherent narrative flow of the canon.
